Privacy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

Dextroverse is a business licensed on the Abu Dhabi mainland (Tajer Emarati licence). We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021). Questions: hello@dextroverse.io.

What we collect

When you request a part on our website, or contact us about one, we keep:

  • your name;
  • your phone or WhatsApp number;
  • your email address;
  • what you wrote about the part, and any photos or files you attach.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request: to quote, make and deliver the part, and to contact you about it.
  • Marketing, only if you ask for it: we send you news or offers only if you tick the separate box for it and then confirm by clicking the link we send you. You can stop at any time by replying "stop" or emailing us.

How long we keep it

If we never get to work on a request, we delete it after 180 days. If you order, we keep the order and invoice records for as long as UAE law requires for business records.

Where it is stored

On our own server in the UAE. Email to us passes through our email provider, and WhatsApp messages through WhatsApp.

Who sees it

Only Dextroverse staff, to handle your request. We never sell your data, and we do not share it with other businesses for their marketing.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email hello@dextroverse.io from the address you used, or tell us the phone number you gave. We reply within 30 days.
