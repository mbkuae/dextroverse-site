Privacy

Tinkerbell Mail is a private tool used by one person, Mohamed Alqubaisi, with his own Gmail account (mohamedbinkaram@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does: sends emails from that account and reads attachments from that account's own mail, so he can forward guest check-in documents to building management.

What it keeps: a sign-in token stored on his own computer. Nothing is sent to or stored on any other server, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Mail content is processed only on his computer.

To remove access: Google Account > Security > Third-party access > Tinkerbell Mail > Remove access.
