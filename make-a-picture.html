Make a picture of your idea with AI

No photo of the part? Ask an AI tool for a picture, then add it to your request. We read the shape from it. You tell us the sizes.

Step 1: Pick a tool

Any of these works: ChatGPT (image creation), Gemini, Midjourney, Copilot Designer. Use the one you already have.

Step 2: Copy this prompt and fill the brackets

A simple product design image of [what it is, e.g. a dashboard phone holder] on a plain white background. One object only. Show it from the side, straight on, with even light and no shadows. No text, no logos, no people. Plain shapes, no tiny details.

Step 3: Ask for more views

Send a second message:

Now show the same object from the top and from the front, same style.

Three views (side, top, front) tell us the most.

Step 4: Add what a picture cannot show

Write in the box what it is for, where it goes, its sizes in millimetres (length, width, height, hole sizes), and the colour you want.

Example: “Holder for a 7 inch screen, 180 mm wide, 60 mm deep, clips onto a 3 mm dashboard edge.”

Step 5: Upload and check

Add 1 to 3 pictures. We read the shape, say what we could not tell, and read the sizes back to you. You confirm before anything is made.

What works best

Boxes, holders, brackets, stands, plates, covers and simple housings.

Ask first about logos and branded designs, people, faces, very fine or organic detail, and anything that has to take heat. For heat, we suggest the right material.

Keep in mind

A picture shows the shape, not the size. Pictures made by AI are guesses, so we never use them to set a size. Only the sizes you confirm count.
