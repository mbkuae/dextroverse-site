How it works

Five steps, all online. Sign in with your email or Google.

  1. Describe it

    In your own words: what it fits, what broke, what it has to do. No drawing needed.

  2. Check the numbers

    We read every size back to you. Nothing is designed until you confirm.

  3. See the price

    The price comes from the real print file of your part. If a cost behind it is not confirmed yet, we tell you rather than guess.

  4. A person approves it

    One of our team checks and signs off every part before it is printed.

  5. Printed and delivered

    Your part arrives with its record: what you asked for, what we checked, and who approved it.

What we promise

You check every number first

We read back every size and hole before we design anything. A misheard number is caught by you in seconds, not by a part that does not fit.

The price comes from the real print file

We price the exact file that will be printed. If we cannot confirm a cost, we tell you rather than guess.

A person approves every part

Nothing goes to the printer until one of our team has checked it and signed it off.

Every part comes with its record

What you asked for, what we measured on the design, anything we could not check, and who approved it.
