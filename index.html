Engineering

Describe a product. We engineer it.

We find the real parts, design the product around them and check every size with you before anything is made.

  1. 1

    We find real parts

    For each part you need to buy, we search UAE sellers first and list real products with a link when we find one. Each part shows its status: found, with its link, or flagged for you to choose.

  2. 2

    We design around them

    Your product is built as one multi-part model around the sizes of those parts, then checked for fit, clearance and printer fit.

  3. 3

    You watch it come together

    Open the exploded view and play the build step by step. The parts list updates as the research runs, with the price of the whole build.

  4. 4

    Assembly manual Coming

    A step-by-step guide with a cut list, a print list and a picture for every step.

The solar dog house

Built in FreeCAD from a short brief and refined in live sessions: 22 parts, 11 printed and 11 bought. It has a vaulted shell on a raised floor, a roof panel on a printed bracket, a sealed electronics box, a fan, a light, a feeder tray and an air-conditioner mount. Each printed part is checked against the printer sizes, and parts too big to print in one piece are flagged to split. In this first run the sizes of the bought parts were placeholders; the research step replaces them with real listings.
